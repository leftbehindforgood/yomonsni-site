What is a Yomonsni Massage?

AYomonsni is a sensual indulgence for your entire being. Rather than simply working on your body, Yomonsni works on three layers of an individual: mind, body, and self. In terms of a massage, means that focus is given to your creativity, sense of connection, stressors in life, as well as to your body. Addressing all the interrelated facets of you that are in need of relaxation enables a deeper and longer lasting state of relaxation within you.

Other typical massage sessions, working just on your body, last for 60-90 minutes, while a Yomonsni massage booking is typically 4-5 hours in length. This extra time is needed to really immerse yourself in the relaxation.

Yomonsni massages are available as single sessions, or, to maximize your ependiture and the benefits to you of 'mind' and 'self' massage work, you can also obtain them by subscription, entitling you to one session per fortnight..
