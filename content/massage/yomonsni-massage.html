What is a Yomonsni massage, and how is it different than anything else you've experienced? The biggest difference one may notice is that other types of massage really only work on your body. While often performed in a peaceful setting, or just a quiet room, the focus of most massage is completely on your body. A Yomonsni massage not only works to relax your body, it also works to relax your mind, to help you to deal with the stressors in life that linger in your mind and manifest as the tense muscles and other issues occuring in your body.
