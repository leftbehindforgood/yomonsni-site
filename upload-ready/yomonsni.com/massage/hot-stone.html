Why hot stones?

Examining the modern history of massage, the addition of hot stones is suprisingly very recent. Use of hot stones has spread widely and one can find a hot stone massage in many locales around the world at this point. Use of heat in a massage is almost intuitive, as the very common practice of using heat pads to soothe sore muscle is widespread.

 

Using a heated stone as a tool in massage allows this heat to be applied all over your body, wherever it is needed or desired. Adding the heat gives a wonderful addition to the pleasure and the relaxation. The unyielding stones also enable a deeper pressure to penetrate to the base of your muscles.

 

Stones are heated in a water bath for a couple of hours before use, allowing heat to penetrate deeply into them. Even after the ston has cooled enough to handle, this core heat will radiate forth and will produce a soft warmness for you when left on your body. After a bit, they are returned to the water bath to warm up again.

 

 

Stones

Primarily eschewing commercially prepared stones, naturally sourced stones are used. The variety of shapes and textures is conducive to diversity of sensation, and some of the rougher stones perform a gentle exfoliation.

 

All stones are deeply washed between sessions, and no stones or equipment is reused between clients without being subjected to a deep clean.
